The commitments

Data-ink. Every drop of ink should present data or be erased. The ratio is a direction to travel, not a score to maximize; a few gridlines that speed lookup have earned their place.

Small multiples. Repeat one design across slices on shared scales so comparisons happen within the eyespan, without memory or interaction.

Integration of text and graphics. Words, numbers and images belong together. Labels sit on the data, explanation sits beside it, and the legend is a last resort.

Sparklines. Intense, simple, word-sized. The context supplies the axes, so the graphic can shrink to the size of the number it replaces.
