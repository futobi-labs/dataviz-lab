Data Viz Lab

Years of building pipelines, warehouses and star schemas, looked at again with a visualization person's eye. The main room draws the things a data engineer actually stares at. The four smaller rooms are studies in craft: strange D3, Stephen Few's discipline, Edward Tufte's ink, and what came after them.

House rules

Synthetic unless stated. Data is generated in-page with a seeded random source, so every reload is the same picture. Real public-domain data is labeled where used.

Color does a job. Identity, magnitude, polarity or status. One fixed categorical order, checked for color-vision deficiency in light and dark.

One axis. No dual-scale charts anywhere in the lab, including the experimental room.

No build step. Plain HTML, CSS and D3 v7.9.0 vendored in the repo. Push to main and it publishes.
