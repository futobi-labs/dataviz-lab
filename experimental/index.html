What this shelf is for

the rule
  • 01

    The question

    The other shelves ask how to show data well. This one asks what else a dataset can become, and what survives the trip.

  • 02

    The rule

    Every mark is bound to a value: no decorative noise, no random seeds passing as signal.

  • 03

    The honesty

    Each page names what it encodes, what it distorts, and the plain chart that would answer the same question faster.

  • 04

    The status

    Treat them as sketches. Some will migrate into the stricter shelves once the honest part is found; most will stay here because they are fun.
