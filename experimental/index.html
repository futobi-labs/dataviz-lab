What this room is for

The other rooms ask how to show data well. This one asks what else a dataset can become, and what survives the trip. The rule is that every mark is bound to a value: no decorative noise, no random seeds passing as signal. The forms are chosen for their pull, not their precision, so each page names what it encodes, what it distorts, and the plain chart that would answer the same question faster.

Treat them as sketches. Some of these ideas will migrate into the stricter rooms once the honest part is found; most will stay here because they are fun.
