What Few insists on

Purpose first. Decide whether the reader looks up values or looks for a pattern before choosing a table or a graph, then pick the graph from the relationship: ranking, time series, part to whole, deviation, distribution, correlation.

Encode for accuracy. Position on a common scale before length, length before angle or area. Bars start at zero. Lines carry time. No 3D, no gauges, no pies.

Mute the context. Gridlines, axes and most data in light gray. Saturated color is an alarm and is spent only on what needs action, with a label so it survives grayscale.

One screen, at a glance. A dashboard is for monitoring. If it scrolls, needs a legend to decode, or decorates, it has failed before anyone reads it.
